Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Overview

Equal is designed to be a simple unit converter. We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little information as possible.

Information we collect

Equal does not require an account and does not collect personal information directly from you.

Information entered into Equal

Values you enter into Equal are used to perform conversions on your device. Equal does not send those values to us for the purpose of providing the conversion.

Purchases

If you purchase an optional feature in Equal, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card or other payment credentials.

Analytics and tracking

Equal does not use advertising trackers or sell personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new revision date.
